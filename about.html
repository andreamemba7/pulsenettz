About Pulse Network

Community knowledge. Practical action. Lasting change.

Pulse Network is a Tanzanian non-profit organization founded in 2018, working at the intersection of environmental sustainability, climate action and social equity. We focus on translating knowledge and policy into practical action at community level, communities should not simply receive solutions; they should help define them, lead them and benefit from them.

Four thematic areas, one purpose

Our vision

A just, resilient and sustainable society where communities have the knowledge, opportunities and power to shape solutions for their environment and future.

Our mission

To empower communities, particularly youth and women, with knowledge, skills, platforms and practical solutions that advance environmental sustainability, climate resilience, gender equality and inclusive development.

What we do

Four thematic areas.

Waste Management & Circularity

Waste separation · Composting · Resource recovery · Circular economy · Green livelihoods · Plastic reduction

Water Management, Sanitation & Hygiene

Water conservation · WASH education · Sanitation · Hygiene · Climate-resilient WASH · Community participation

Climate Change Literacy & Action

Climate literacy · Adaptation · Climate justice · Youth education · Community dialogues · Climate communication

Gender Equality & Social Inclusion

Women's leadership · Gender-responsive programming · Youth and girls · Inclusion · Gender and climate justice
